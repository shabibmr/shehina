New User : Equity Investor.

Equity Investors are who invest in Shehnat. The money goes to the Shehnat expenses and treasury.
Currently the plan is for 10 investors with 1% Equity. The investors picked are mostly importers.
These investors have a different method for calculating.
The shipments by these investors or shipments to these investors (they are the Buyers).
So, the current vendors of these investor companies will have an option to not invest any Manager Capital. the treasury fund will be used 100%.
On such transactions the treasury profit will be 75% given back to the investor and only 25% is moved to Reserve funds.
