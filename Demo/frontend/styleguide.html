Icons

Duotone, 24px grid: a green primary layer and a gold secondary layer. Inactive icons are stone with gold at 40%. The gold layer never carries meaning alone.

Motion

Expressive and choreographed, but every piece has a job: it shows where you came from, what just changed, or that money moved. Use the Motion switch at the top to preview full motion or the reduced fallbacks, whatever your device is set to.
