# Studios and writeups: mobile layout and style systems, 2024–2026

Scope kept to pages opened in this pass (or, where noted, a search-index extract of a primary URL that did not re-render). Cutoff 2026-10-31. Award galleries were not used as evidence. No project below was invented to fill a studio on the seed list.

## Which studios published mobile case studies in 2024, 2025, or 2026 with enough layout detail to name one structural move?

### Takeaway

The writeups that actually name a structural move are platform design systems, not the agency portfolios on the seed list. Google’s Material 3 Expressive (phone and Wear OS, 2025) and Apple’s Liquid Glass / iOS 26 guidance (2025) each specify how layout, shape, and motion share one job. Among studios, Work & Co’s Blue Bottle app (September 2025) is the clearest shipped phone case: navigation is cut to three anchors and the wait state becomes an editorial surface. Grab’s Duxton story documents a mobile system that expects layouts to change by country, but it does not publish a visual style. Metalab’s Atoms case states a hard layout constraint (three habits) and is wellness, not fintech; the page does not date the work.

### Cited Findings

- **Google Material Design — Material 3 Expressive, phone, May 2025 (system, not a studio skin).** Announced as an update to Material 3, explicitly not a new version and not “M4”; M3 is not deprecated. Research cited on the system blog and on design.google: 46 studies, more than 18,000 participants. The named layout move in the email-app study is moving **Send** out of the top app bar (where it sat beside secondary controls such as attach) and making it a larger button just above the keyboard, drawn in a secondary color. The same essay names the tactics that are supposed to work together: color, shape, size, motion, and containment, including a studied floating toolbar and tests of how large a button can get before it overwhelms the screen. — [design.google research essay](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research); “not M4” and the 46-study / 18,000-participant line also appear in the indexed text of [Building with M3 Expressive](https://m3.material.io/blog/building-with-m3-expressive) (direct fetch of that blog returned only the title; the research essay, which links to it, was read in full).
- **Google — same system, shape and motion specs, May 2025.** Shape page: 35 new shapes, built-in shape morph, corner-radius tokens raised to large increased 20dp, extra large increased 32dp, extra extra large 48dp, and fully rounded corners retokenized as `full` instead of 50% of the component. Shapes are meant to echo the roundness of the type (Google Sans Flex). Motion page (indexed extract, not a full re-fetch): physics springs replace easing-plus-duration; an Expressive scheme overshoots and is for hero moments; a Standard scheme has minimal bounce and is for utilitarian products; spatial springs (position, orientation, size, shape) may overshoot, effect springs (color, opacity) must not. — [M3 Shape](https://m3.material.io/styles/shape/overview-principles); [M3 motion physics](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview/how-it-works); [motion theming note](https://m3.material.io/blog/m3-expressive-motion-theming).
- **Google — Wear OS Material 3 Expressive, 25 August 2025.** Structural move is to stop using a rectangular layout on a round screen. Edge-hugging buttons follow the bottom arc. `TransformingLazyColumn` shrinks row width as items approach the top and bottom edge. Tiles use a 3-slot `PrimaryLayout`. Non-scrolling screens (media, confirmations) use breakpoints and pagination so one task fills the glance. Shipped examples named in the post include Phone (incoming-call edge button), Alarms toggles, Google Maps tile, and media controls with a wavy progress ring. — [Android Developers Blog, 25 August 2025](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/08/introducing-material-3-expressive-for-wear-os.html).
- **Apple Human Interface — Liquid Glass, previewed 9 June 2025 across iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS Tahoe 26, watchOS 26, and tvOS 26.** Structural move: controls, toolbars, and navigation are a distinct functional layer floating above the content layer, redrawn so they are concentric with the rounded corners of the hardware instead of a rectangular inset. On iPhone, the tab bar shrinks while the user scrolls down and expands when they scroll back up, so content takes the screen without navigation leaving. Sidebars on iPad and Mac refract the content behind them. Lock Screen time is Liquid Glass set in San Francisco, with weight, width, and height of each numeral scaled to sit behind the photo subject. — [Apple Newsroom, 9 June 2025](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-introduces-a-delightful-and-elegant-new-software-design/); [HIG Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials).
- **Work & Co (Accenture Song) — Blue Bottle Coffee mobile app, news post 9 September 2025; design-director interview 3 November 2025.** Shipped iOS and Android (Flutter). Structural move, in the client’s words: the redesign “trades typical app navigation for three clear points of interaction — café location, order, and our iconic Blue Bottle logo.” The second move is temporal: the gap between order and pickup is not a status spinner; it is Morning Notes, an editorial slot (art with the story of the piece, neighborhood spotlights, brand and brewing notes, lyrics). Category: commerce / hospitality, not fintech. — [Work & Co, 9 September 2025](https://www.work.co/news/blue-bottle-launches-new-mobile-app/); [Built In interview with Myhra Mirza, 3 November 2025](https://builtin.com/articles/inside-reimagined-blue-bottle-coffee-mobile-app). Built In says the Q&A was edited for length.
- **Grab, in-house — Duxton design system for the passenger app.** Established as the default system in late 2023; by the second half of 2025 roughly 50% of the consumer app (more than 5 million lines of code) used Duxton components via an SDK. The layout claim they do state: a layout that fits Thailand may have to change entirely for Indonesia, and color, corner radius, and iconography stay configurable so a component can flex locally without leaving the system. This is a mobile system writeup (passenger app: rides and food). It is not a palette, type, or motion spec. — [Figma customer story](https://www.figma.com/customers/how-grab-scales-hyperlocal-experiences-across-southeast-asia-with-figma-and-ai/). The page itself has no visible publication date; the 2023 and H2 2025 dates are in the body.
- **Metalab — Atoms, the Atomic Habits app (wellness).** Page states no year. Structural move: unlike habit apps that allow unlimited goals, Atoms allows only three at a time, and a new habit stays locked until the user makes progress on the ones they have. Creation asks both the habit and the identity reason (“the what and the why”). Snooze and frequency changes are specified as layout-level escapes so the three-slot limit does not break when life does. Results claimed on the page, undated: top 10 and Editors’ Choice within a month of launch, 4.8 stars, 4.6k reviews. — [Metalab, Atoms](https://www.metalab.com/work/atoms).
- **Peer-reviewed check on the Google effect size, CHI 2026.** Abstract on the ACM landing page (full paper not read): 48 participants, 10 applications; expressive variants produced 33% faster time-to-first-fixation (mean 1.60s vs 2.40s) and 20% faster task completion than the previous Material system, with larger effects for participants over 45. The same abstract says earlier Material buttons were a single 48dp height, while Expressive adds sizes from extra small to extra large so a key action can be sized differently from a secondary one. — [CHI 2026 abstract](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3790373).

### Inferences

- “Enough layout detail to name one structural move” filtered out most agency case-study indexes. Metalab, Work & Co, and the others list many mobile products; the pages that survive are the ones that say what was removed, resized, or reassigned (three nav targets, three habits, Send above the keyboard, chrome that shrinks, rows that taper on a circle).
- Grab is the counterexample inside the set: a real mobile system, widely shipped, whose public story is adoption and local override rather than a style. It should not be paraphrased into a visual theme.
- Atoms is the best non-platform wellness example, but only if the report treats the missing date as disqualifying or as unknown. Nothing on the page places it inside 2024–2026.

## For each opened writeup: layout move, stated style rules, where it works, and what they refused

### Takeaway

Where a maker states rules, the rules are prohibitions as often as palettes. Apple forbids glass in the content layer. Google forbids semantic shapes, unlabeled actions, and broken list structures, and splits motion into an expressive scheme and a utilitarian one. Blue Bottle refused a literal port of packaging color and type. Atoms refused the open-ended habit list. Grab refused one layout for every Southeast Asian market, without saying what the pixels are.

### Cited Findings

- **Material 3 Expressive (phone) — style rules they actually state.** Color, shape, size, motion, and containment are the levers; larger size plus a secondary color is how Send becomes findable. Shape: 35-shape library, morph on selection, in-progress actions, and environmental change; corner tokens 20 / 32 / 48dp and `full`; rectangular defaults are fully rounded, with single corners free to become asymmetrical. Type: shapes should share the roundness of Google Sans Flex (no other type scale was on the shape page). Motion: Expressive springs overshoot; Standard springs barely bounce; spatial properties may overshoot; color and opacity may not. Research attributes they say rose: playful, energetic, creative, friendly, and a “positive vibe”; preference for the expressive versions was strongest among 18-to-24-year-olds, up to 87%. Desirability lifts they report: subculture perception +32%, modernity +34%, rebelliousness +30%. — [design.google](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research); [Shape](https://m3.material.io/styles/shape/overview-principles); [motion overview](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview/how-it-works).
- **Where Google says the phone system works, and what it refuses.** Works, in their tests, on an email send action and across 10 apps when the change is size, color, and containment inside a familiar structure: key elements spotted up to four times faster in the expressive email screen; fixation-time age gap between under-45 and 45-plus largely disappeared; larger targets and high-contrast containment helped participants with varying movement and vision. Refusals, in their words: a playlist that replaced the vertical list with scattered album art “looked modern and exciting” and still failed usability; removing text labels from email actions decreased usability; “no amount of expressive design will beat basic functionality”; a strong minority preferred calmer, less intense versions; what works in a media player or email app “might not be suitable for something like a banking interface.” Shape page: “Shape is versatile, not semantic” — do not assign a function to one shape (a wave can mean loading, or it can be a button container); use abstract shapes sparingly; do not trade clarity for the shape; decorative crops and avatars are where shape is allowed to be freer. Motion page: do not use the bouncy scheme on utilitarian products; do not let color or opacity overshoot. — same three URLs as above.
- **Wear OS Expressive — style rules and refusals.** Color: deeper tonal palettes, including tertiary; apps and tiles may follow dynamic color taken from the watch face or keep brand color. The post “especially” recommends dynamic color on tiles so they match neighboring tiles. Type: variable fonts, weight and width axes, plus roles called out on the linked Wear guidance (the blog itself stresses variable axes, not a named size ramp). Shape and motion: components morph; a pressed key grows and the neighbor shrinks; play/pause can differ by shape. Layout rule that is also a refusal: do not treat the watch as a small phone — use the circular canvas (edge-hugging control, tapering list, 3-slot tile) and keep non-scrolling screens to one task. Performance constraint stated up front: expressive UI still has to respect watch battery limits. The gallery alt text says cancel and confirm use different shapes; the prose explains morph as state feedback, not as a code of “this shape means cancel.” — [Wear OS blog, 25 August 2025](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/08/introducing-material-3-expressive-for-wear-os.html).
- **Apple Liquid Glass — style rules they actually state.** Two materials, with different jobs. Liquid Glass is only the functional layer (tab bars, sidebars, controls). Standard materials (on iOS/iPadOS: ultra-thin, thin, regular, thick) structure the content layer. Two glass variants: regular (blur and luminosity shift, for text-heavy surfaces such as alerts, sidebars, popovers) and clear (highly translucent, only over visually rich media). If the media is bright, they specify a dark dimming layer at 35% opacity under clear glass; if the media is already dark, or AVKit is supplying dimming, add none. Color is taken from content and switches between light and dark; do not choose a material because of the color it seems to add, because system settings change it. Type on the Lock Screen: San Francisco numerals with variable weight, width, and height. Motion described qualitatively: controls morph as the user needs more options or changes section; glass reacts to movement with specular highlights. No stiffness, damping, or duration numbers on the pages opened. — [Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-introduces-a-delightful-and-elegant-new-software-design/); [HIG Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials).
- **Where Apple says it works, and what it refuses.** Works as navigation and controls floating over content, and as clear glass over photos and video. The shrink-on-scroll tab bar is specified for iOS so content gets focus while navigation stays reachable. Refusals, quoted from the HIG: “Don’t use Liquid Glass in the content layer” (exception: a transient in-content control such as a slider or toggle may take on glass only while active). Use glass on custom controls sparingly; overuse distracts from content. Do not stack the decision on apparent color. Avoid quaternary vibrancy on thin and ultra-thin materials because contrast is too low. On visionOS, prefer translucency to opaque window fills, which they say makes people feel constricted. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast are acknowledged as settings that change glass appearance; that is an accessibility override, not a second product theme. — [HIG Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials).
- **Blue Bottle / Work & Co — style rules they actually state, and the refusal.** Palette: “a refined color palette anchored by Blue Bottle’s iconic signature blue”; “warm tones replace the stark white of the previous design”; rich photography; “East and West” influences, with one published example being a Japanese woodblock (Yanagawa Shigenobu, “Celebrated Waterfall”) on the wait surface. Navigation structure: three targets (location, order, logo). No type family, size ramp, corner radius, or motion spec is stated. The refusal is the interesting style rule: Mirza says the physical identity could not be copied, because digital type and color had to survive accessibility and legibility constraints; they built a digital style with the brand team instead of transferring packaging. She also frames the visual goal as “minimal yet tactile,” which is still a mood, not a token. Category is commerce. Outcomes in these two posts are qualitative (membership, ritual, hospitality); neither post gives a conversion number. — [Work & Co](https://www.work.co/news/blue-bottle-launches-new-mobile-app/); [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/inside-reimagined-blue-bottle-coffee-mobile-app).
- **Grab Duxton — rules they state, and what is absent.** Stated: one component source; local layouts may be replaced entirely; color, corner radius, and iconography are the properties called out as configurable; local A/B tests are how a market variant earns its place. Not stated: a palette, a typeface, a shape library, a motion scheme, or a screen template. The failure mode they describe is organizational (about 50% adoption, legacy migration, evangelism), not a visual rule that broke. Head of Design Patrick Jean: building the system and SDK is “doable”; scaling it is the hard part. — [Figma / Grab](https://www.figma.com/customers/how-grab-scales-hyperlocal-experiences-across-southeast-asia-with-figma-and-ai/).
- **Atoms / Metalab — rules they state, and what is absent.** The product rule is the layout: three concurrent habits; progress gates the next slot; each habit stores an action and an identity reason; tracking is a large repetition count (“214 votes towards becoming a better me” is the example on the page); periodic reflection prompts; snooze and frequency edits so a missed day is not a failed layout. Brand is described as connecting the book and the app. No palette, type, radius, or motion values are in the text. No failure is admitted. Year is not stated, so “where it works” cannot be tied to 2024–2026 from this page alone. — [Metalab Atoms](https://www.metalab.com/work/atoms).

### Inferences

- The usable pairs for a layout-plus-style system are Google (size and containment do the layout job; shape and spring tokens do the style job, with an explicit off switch for utilitarian products) and Apple (layer separation does the layout job; glass variants and vibrancy do the style job, with an explicit off switch for the content layer). Blue Bottle is the studio example of the same pairing at product scale, with a much thinner style spec: three-item navigation plus a warm-against-blue palette, and a written refusal to ignore legibility.
- Atoms shows a structural move with almost no published style system. It belongs in a “constraint as layout” note, not in a palette survey.
- Grab shows the opposite: a system that is real and mobile, whose public detail stops at “tokens can vary by market.”

### Gaps

- Motion-scheme wording (Expressive vs Standard, spatial vs effect springs) is from the search-index text of the Material motion URLs, not a full page render in this session. Shape rules, the design.google essay, the Wear OS post, both Apple pages, both Blue Bottle pages, the Grab story, and the Atoms case were read directly.
- The CHI 2026 paper’s methods, screen images, and whether the 33% figure is the same study as design.google’s “up to four times” were not verified beyond the abstract.
- Blue Bottle’s three-target bar and Morning Notes are described in prose. The screens themselves were not available as text; exact order of the three controls, type sizes, and the blue hex are unpublished.
- No opened studio page from this window states a motion curve, a radius scale, and a palette together. That combination showed up in the platform documents, not in Instrument / Collins / Pentagram / frog / IDEO and the rest of the seed list.

## Contradictions, non-fintech coverage, web-only exclusions, and unverified claims

### Takeaway

Apple and Google, in the same season, give opposite instructions about chrome: shrink the navigation layer so content dominates, or enlarge the primary action so the eye hits it first. Google also contradicts itself across surfaces and across documents: phone guidance says a shape must not mean a function, while the Wear OS gallery shows cancel and confirm as different shapes, and the marketing essay’s “up to four times faster” is a much larger claim than the CHI 2026 abstract’s 33% faster fixation and 20% faster completion. Non-fintech coverage that survives is commerce (Blue Bottle), wellness (Atoms, undated), mobility and food (Grab, thin visually), and media-or-system examples inside Apple’s and Google’s own apps. Several famous systems are the wrong year or the wrong medium.

### Cited Findings

- **Contradiction — what “focus” means.** Apple, 9 June 2025: when the user scrolls, tab bars shrink “to bring focus to the content,” and glass exists to keep controls visually separate from that content. Google, design.google essay: focus is produced by making the key control larger and moving it into the thumb zone (Send above the keyboard), and they report that this, not a quieter chrome, is what made the control findable. Both claim the user’s attention. They prescribe opposite changes to the control layer. — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-introduces-a-delightful-and-elegant-new-software-design/); [design.google](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research).
- **Contradiction — may form carry meaning?** Apple’s HIG assigns the material a job: Liquid Glass means “this is navigation or a control,” and using it in the content layer is the stated failure (“confusing visual hierarchy”). Google’s phone shape chapter forbids that kind of assignment: “Avoid making shapes literal or assigning a specific function or meaning to a single shape.” The Wear OS post’s gallery alt text then shows “different shapes for cancel and confirm,” which is a semantic use if read strictly. The Wear prose only commits to morph as feedback for state (pressed, play/pause), so the contradiction is between the phone principle and the watch gallery, not a written Wear rule that cancel must be a given silhouette. — [HIG Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials); [M3 Shape](https://m3.material.io/styles/shape/overview-principles); [Wear OS blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/08/introducing-material-3-expressive-for-wear-os.html).
- **Contradiction — one motion personality or two.** Google publishes two schemes and tells utilitarian products to refuse bounce, and the research essay separately warns expressive treatment off a banking interface. Apple’s 2025 announcement presents one material and one morphing behavior across phone, tablet, desktop, watch, and TV, with the split drawn by layer (chrome vs content) and by accessibility settings, not by product category. — [motion overview](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview/how-it-works); [design.google](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research); [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-introduces-a-delightful-and-elegant-new-software-design/).
- **Contradiction — effect size inside Google’s own account.** design.google: participants “spot key UI elements up to four times faster,” illustrated by the email Send button, and tap time “decreased by seconds.” CHI 2026 abstract, same general claim (10 apps, expressive vs previous Material, eye tracking): mean fixation 33% faster (1.60s vs 2.40s) and tasks 20% faster, n=48. “Up to four times” can be a maximum while 33% is a mean; that reading was not confirmed because the paper body was not opened. The two numbers should not be collapsed into one stat. — [design.google](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research); [CHI 2026 abstract](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3790373).
- **Contradiction — sameness across contexts, including a pre-window note.** Grab, writing about a live phone app: a layout that works in one Southeast Asian market may need to be replaced in another; radius and color are allowed to move. Historical Airbnb DLS (Karri Saarinen; the system dates from about 2016, not from this window): most components were deliberately the same on iOS and Android, with exceptions only for navigation, system iconography, contextual actions, and interactions, plus a few layout rules for tablet. Spotify’s Encore essay of 31 August 2023, also outside the window, argues the other way from Airbnb: a single platform-agnostic kit had become too flexible, so they added platform subsystems and spacing variables that produce “layout themes” for a button family. Useful only as context for the Grab claim. — [Grab](https://www.figma.com/customers/how-grab-scales-hyperlocal-experiences-across-southeast-asia-with-figma-and-ai/); [Saarinen, DLS](https://karrisaarinen.com/dls/); [Figma Blog, Spotify, 31 August 2023](https://www.figma.com/blog/creating-coherence-how-spotifys-design-system-goes-beyond-platforms/).
- **Non-fintech coverage that was actually opened.** Commerce: Blue Bottle. Wellness: Atoms (undated) and the Wear OS water-intake tile as a system example, not a studio case. Mobility and food: Grab’s passenger app. Media and culture, only as surfaces inside the platform stories: Apple names Apple Music, Apple News, and Apple Podcasts as apps receiving the new navigation layer; Google’s negative example is a music playlist and its positive example is email; Morning Notes on Blue Bottle is an editorial module inside a commerce app, not a news product. No opened 2024–2026 case from a publisher (newsroom or magazine) documented a phone layout system.
- **Web-only or wrong-medium, not forced.** Metalab Headspace: web. Shopify Polaris 2025: web components for admin, checkout, accounts, and POS, without a layout essay. Metalab’s own site case study on Awwwards (28 June 2024) is the studio website, frames-as-motion, not a product app. Work & Co IKEA text credits an e-commerce app and a design system but cites 2020 sales growth; not placed in 2024–2026. PGA TOUR Vision: visionOS. Pentagram Fortuny: an older iPhone fabric catalog, not this window.

### Inferences

- A report that treats “2025 mobile style” as one trend will blur two incompatible structures: Apple’s receding functional layer and Google’s enlarged, contained primary action. They can share a phone and still not share a layout idea. Glass, blur, and spring are the surface both are using; the layout decision underneath is the part worth keeping, which is why the glass look alone was not treated as the finding.
- Studio marketing in this window often stops at hospitality, personalization, or “a design system of N components.” Those counts (for example Metalab’s older Ro writeup, which was not re-opened here) do not name a layout move. Blue Bottle is the exception because the client stated the three anchors.
- The seed list of brand and product studios is a weak source for specified mobile style systems in 2024–2026. The specified systems are Google’s and Apple’s, plus Grab’s incomplete public account of Duxton.

### Gaps

- Atoms’ ship year was not on the case study and was not looked up elsewhere. If it shipped before 2024, it is out of window despite being the cleanest wellness layout constraint found.
- Suno’s gradient system and “design the kit, not every generated screen” claim were not re-read on a rendered page.
- TouchTunes and Vivo were not opened; they might add social and telecom cases if a later pass reads [the TouchTunes note](https://work.co/news/touchtunes-launches-new-app) and [the Vivo note](https://work.co/news/como-a-vivo-esta-reposicionando-sua-estrategia-digital/).
- No primary 2024–2026 essay was opened for Uber Base, Airbnb’s current mobile UI, or a news publisher’s app. Early Airbnb DLS and the 2023 Spotify essay are context only.
- Whether iOS 26’s public release (as opposed to the 9 June 2025 preview) changed the shrink-on-scroll rule was not checked against later HIG diffs. A search extract of the Materials page mentioned a 9 September 2025 changelog line, “Updated guidance for Liquid Glass”; that line was below the portion read in full.
